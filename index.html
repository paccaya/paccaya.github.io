ryan chan's

talking cobweb space


Something is awry
when the sky
is falling

the sky is falling
because we want it
to be bright

perhaps the heat
collapsing upon us now
is calling upon
our inner heat

The beds of coal
of our culture
that we have
forgotten we walk upon

our feet are burnt
and ashen
and yet we wonder
why our roots,
where we sit and stand,
bring no nutrient

the sky is falling
precisely because we are
falling not in love
but in collapse

As Bayo Akomolafe once said: "We often speak about getting lost in the dark, but it is also possible to get lost in light. ... Name the color, blind the eye."
