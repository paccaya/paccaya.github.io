Age

By Ryan Chan

11 Oct 2026 11:50 PM

Your words are
origami
they come with age

Each crease telling me
what
I need to know

Am I just someone who does
not
know any better?

Am I just the one who
needs
to know how to do things when
you
are gone?

I wish you could cut the
carrots
of your concepts

and feed me
without
airplanes

Am I just a
child
to you?

Helpless and in
need
of teaching?
