Moon(shine)

By Ryan Chan

24 Oct 2026 11:14 PM

Laying down on
the stone porch
thoughts pirouette

The night in ballet
even
with the repellant
of light
artificial

This contrast,
light and dark,
so suggestive
under the moon

Dark

its expanse only
known in the pockets
of stars
that make,
all that is dark,
light

Visible

I write by candlelight,
her fire flickering
so do I

Light

I light my pencil on fire

I blow it out.

pencil tip burnt, wood black

Invisible

what is not there?
