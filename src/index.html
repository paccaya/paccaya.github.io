ryan chan's

talking cobweb space


Something is a amiss
when the tears of the
world are not honored

the abyss is ever open
we are falling yet
we forget the falling
is grounding

in times of descent,
one is called to dive

So, I open to how
this world may be
in much death and
still bearing life

We the eyes that bring
fluid to this Earth
I cry, animating loss

because the tears of the
world are not mine
but all the others

Susan Griffin once wrote, "At the center of / all my sorrows / I have felt a presence / that was not mine alone."
