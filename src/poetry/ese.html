I am the lichen residing on rocks,
as we are the soil showered with nutrients.

I am the unrelenting storms,
as we are beaten into tight-knit community.

I am the waves crashing,
as we are the land being shaped into connection.

I am the lightning unraveling,
as we are fire kindled and enlivened.

I am the air being exhaled,
as we are pampered by fauna's breath.

I am gravity holding the weight of all,
as we are embraced into rest upon our feet.

I am the rain everlasting,
as we are the watered into rhapsody.

I am the one being read,
as we are in a joyous marriage of reading.

I am excruciating pain,
as we are then balmed by compassion.

I am the canopy of shade,
as we are softened from the scintillating sun.

I am graciously affectionate,
as we receive everything from divine relationship.

I am the seeds of loss and despair,
as our dreams are not born out of hope
but grief
and love.
