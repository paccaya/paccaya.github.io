A Silent Spring is upon us.
Yet, this time
it has to do
with sound.
Of the rational
deadening the wilds.
It has to do
with the seasonality
of soul
seasons so out of balance
to the pulsing eurhythmics
of life.

So, we dance. So, we dance.

So, we sing. So, we sing.

So, we choose silence to listen:
to where all the wild things are.
