Once upon a time, there was a peoples without a place.
They lived on something sometimes called earth.
These peoples ate, shit, and slept.
They wept, too. And they Sang.

They Sang in reciprocation to Skywoman.
In breathing, heartful syncopation.
Ba-dum
Ba-dum
Ba-dum

Wind swooned in circulation. The breath of life
now was animated in land-bodies.

They Sang in reciprocation,
yet something started to go missing.
People went missing. Not in death
or anything dark but in Sky.

Sky was all they knew,
there was a time when they forgot Song.
they looked to sky, "Wherefore did I lose you?"
The sky said nothing.
For a Sky does not speak.
In that emptiness, hunger grew.

Hunger grew so much that the peoples looked up.
They only knew "up" because that was where the sky was.
In feverish thirst, they ripped apart the fabric of the sky.
To find its creator.

This is what the peoples did.
They spoke for the sky as
the sky could not speak
Sky was "out there."

Sky in tears. The sky was torn.
So, grief came.
In great waves.
In the loss of many things.
Yet, this only upset the peoples.

The people's history rewrote Sky.

It was during the time of
this some time that song was lost
because Sky is now called 'atmosphere.'

flying carriages filled the Sky
people wanted to get places
for their homes no longer
were their bodies.

but in 'safe spaces'
away from things 'too close from home'
especially bodies and their feelings.

for not all 'feelings' were a place
more than a State of pain.

As in the rewriting of Sky,
something was lost
and that something we
have been trying to find.

yet in our search
we have been nothing
but Lost

loss itself
forgetting its own breath
the breath that connects
us to all that is Other.

for it is a symptom
of a peoples without a place
to create a Race out of life
always to get somewhere.

some place other than here.

the Sky was suffocating
at most here, in the fear
that its breath became work
collapsing lungs
that no longer Sang
as much speak.

Sky is available only
for those who can eke (eek)
to eke out a breath
at most here.
