--- title: "To mother" layout: layouts/writing.njk author: Ryan Chan description: draft: false date: 2026-11-13T19:18:00-04:00 ---

I remember you
yes, I really do

it comes in the tears
a reality torn
when you are not there
at the dinner table

the tears of grief
when I see my friends
take for granted a guardian's
presence

guarded from their love
in wanting to defend the self
or their mothers
from a burdening presence

oh, yes, I remember the shame
in that: the burden of another's presence

co-parenting this intense longing to be seen
as strong and capable

as that was who I wanted to be
when I was younger

Now, I am brought to tears
reflecting on that callous connection

I tear up when I remember,
again and again, how your condition
was concealed by father and many others
in protection of me,

me, as a kid, who could not
metabolize death
in its gravity of weight and heaviness

I know you did not want me to fall
but I did

in how I barely knew what happened
until recently as my curiousity is making
clear to me how you, as a parent,
died long before

how you could only relate
to me as a child

someone to be educated
and someone who does not know any better

I know you wanted
to protect me from pain
but I wish I knew you
went through the mother
of all surgeries

I wish I knew
all the unraveling uncertainty
that you went through
with dad bedside

I wish I knew how close
you were to going so I
did not have to leave the
middle of a class to learn
of your condition

I wish you, as a parent,
could relate to me as a person;
not a kid

I kid you not
people have capacities
of holding pain and depth
more than the compulsion of
self-protection may console you

since your defense
in protecting me
in all the shields of silence
only made it clear how
you mistrusted me

I wish I could have been
somebody to you —
not just a child in need of protection

because your silence hurts twice
in every experience where I recognize
how much has been withheld from me
just to protect me

I am in distress
because I knew you meant well
but I was dying under your defense

I do not want to be
defended from things
based on who I was to you

I know you never left me
evenin your longing to protect me
as I still feel the grief
in praise of you

A mother who wanted to protect me
